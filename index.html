Our Values

We believe it is expected of us to carry out the five British values which are:

  • Democracy
  • The rule of law
  • Individual liberty
  • Mutual respect
  • Respect and tolerance
Learn more about our values

What do they mean?

Fundamental British values, defined by the government and heavily promoted in schools, include democracy, the rule of law, individual liberty, mutual respect and tolerance for those with different faiths and beliefs. They are designed to promote unity, safety and a shared understanding of citizenship.
