Our City, Our Community

Westminster City Council works to make Westminster a safe, clean, prosperous and inclusive place for everyone who lives, works and visits our city.

About Westminster City Council

Westminster City Council is the local authority for the City of Westminster. We are responsible for delivering a wide range of services that help our community thrive, including housing, education, transport, waste and recycling, social care, and community safety.

Our vision is to build a fairer, greener and more prosperous Westminster — a city where everyone has the opportunity to live, work and enjoy life.
